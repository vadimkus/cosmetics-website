## 1. Power Solutions Overview

All Power Solution ampoules are **2 ml × 10 vials** per box, sterile, and formulated specifically for transdermal delivery via microneedling. They share a common base of **sh-Polypeptide-7** (human growth factor–like peptide) and are free of parabens, ethanol, artificial fragrance, artificial pigment, and sulfate.

| Code | Full Name | Primary Action | Key Actives | Best For |
|------|-----------|---------------|-------------|----------|
| **AWS** | Power Solution AWS | Anti-wrinkle | Acetyl Hexapeptide-8, Ceramide, Adenosine, Copper Tripeptide-1 | Fine lines, crow's feet, forehead lines |
| **SWS** | Power Solution SWS | Anti-pigmentation | Arbutin, Kojic Acid, Licorice Root Extract | Melasma, sun spots, post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation |

| **CVS** | Power Solution CVS | Revitalization | Botanical Stem Cells, Lactobacillus Ferments, Panthenol | Dull skin, post-treatment recovery, sensitivity |
| **CTS** | Power Solution CTS | Skin remodeling | Collagen, Copper Tripeptide-1, Palmitoyl Hexapeptide-12 | Laxity, loss of firmness, skin thinning |

### How They Work with Microneedling

Microneedling creates thousands of micro-channels in the skin (depth 0.25–1.5 mm). These channels increase transdermal absorption of active ingredients by up to **300×** compared to topical application alone. The Power Solution ampoules are designed to exploit this window — they are applied **during** the needling pass so that actives are delivered directly into the dermis where they can stimulate fibroblasts, inhibit melanogenesis, or regulate sebum production at the cellular level.

## 2. Supporting GENOSYS Products

### Carboxy Therapy — EZ CO₂ MASK KIT

The cornerstone pre-treatment that **primes the skin** before microneedling:

- **Mechanism:** Gel + mask contact produces CO₂ micro-particles → triggers the **Bohr effect** → hemoglobin releases O₂ into tissue → increased local blood flow and oxygenation
- **Benefits:** Improved product absorption, enhanced cellular metabolism, brightening, firming
- **Clinical Testing:** Tested for improving skin roughness and hyperpigmentation
- **Application:** Apply gel to skin, place mask sheet, leave 15–20 minutes, remove and wipe clean
- **Format:** Gel 20 g × 5 + Mask 12 g × 5 (5 treatments per box)

## 3. General Microneedling Guidelines

### Needle Depth Reference

| Area | Depth | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| Forehead | 0.5 – 1.0 mm | Avoid temples (superficial vessels) |
| Crow's feet | 0.25 – 0.5 mm | Thin skin — gentle passes |
| Cheeks | 1.0 – 1.5 mm | Thickest facial skin, tolerates deeper needling |
| Nose | 0.25 – 0.5 mm | Thin skin over cartilage |
| Upper lip | 0.5 – 0.75 mm | Sensitive — numb well |
| Chin | 0.5 – 1.0 mm | Moderate depth |
| Neck | 0.25 – 0.5 mm | Very thin skin — light pressure only |
| Décolleté | 0.25 – 0.5 mm | Very thin skin — light pressure only |

### Standard Session Flow

Every protocol below follows this general flow:

```
CLEANSE → PREP → [CARBOXY if indicated] → MICRONEEDLE + AMPOULE → MASK → FINISH
```

### Time Budgeting

| Step | Duration |
|------|----------|
| Cleansing & prep | 5–10 min |
| Carboxy (EZ CO₂) | 15–20 min |
| Microneedling pass | 15–25 min |
| Post-treatment mask | 15–30 min |
| Finishing & SPF | 5 min |
| **Total** | **55–90 min** |

## Protocol 1 — Anti-Wrinkle

**Indication:** Fine lines, moderate wrinkles, loss of firmness, photoaging
**Frequency:** Every 3–4 weeks | **Course:** 4–6 sessions
**Primary Ampoule:** POWER SOLUTION **AWS**
**Support Ampoule (optional combo):** POWER SOLUTION **CTS** (alternate sessions for collagen remodeling)

### Step-by-Step

| Step | Product | Technique | Duration |
|------|---------|-----------|----------|
| **1. Double Cleanse** | SNOW O₂ CLEANSER (500 ml) | Apply to dry face, let O₂ bubbles activate, massage, rinse | 5 min |
| **2. Tone** | SNOW BOOSTER (1000 ml) | Saturate gauze, wipe entire face to balance pH | 2 min |
| **3. Carboxy Therapy** | EZ CO₂ MASK KIT | Apply gel evenly → place mask sheet → leave on | 15–20 min |
| **4. Remove & Prep** | Clean gauze | Remove mask, gently wipe residual gel. Do NOT rinse — leave active residue | 2 min |
| **5. Microneedling** | POWER SOLUTION **AWS** (1–2 vials) | Open vial, apply half to face. Needle at 0.5–1.0 mm. Apply remaining product during passes. Work in zones: forehead → temples → cheeks → perioral → chin | 15–20 min |
| **6. Post Mask** | PEPTIDE GEL MASK | Apply immediately post-needling — thermo-sensitive gel cools on contact, delivers Acetyl Hexapeptide-8 and Collagen | 20–30 min |
| **7. Finish** | SOOTHING REPAIR POSTCREAM | Thin layer over entire treatment area | 2 min |
| **8. SPF** | ULTRA SHIELD SUN CREAM SPF 50+ | Apply before discharge (if daytime) | 1 min |

### Why This Works

- **Carboxy** increases dermal oxygenation, prepping fibroblasts for stimulation
- **AWS** delivers Acetyl Hexapeptide-8 (Argireline — relaxes micro-contractions), Ceramide (barrier repair), Adenosine (proven anti-wrinkle), and Copper Tripeptide-1 (stimulates collagen I & III synthesis)
- **Peptide Gel Mask** continues peptide delivery while soothing inflammation
- **Alternating CTS** every other session adds direct Collagen and Palmitoyl Hexapeptide-12 for deeper remodeling

## Protocol 2 — Anti-Pigmentation

**Indication:** Melasma, sun spots, post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH), uneven skin tone
**Frequency:** Every 2–3 weeks | **Course:** 6–8 sessions
**Primary Ampoule:** POWER SOLUTION **SWS**
**Needle Depth:** 0.25–0.5 mm (shallower than anti-wrinkle — melanocytes sit at the dermo-epidermal junction)

> **Important:** Aggressive needling on pigmented skin can worsen PIH. Use conservative depths and moderate speed.

### Step-by-Step

| Step | Product | Technique | Duration |
|------|---------|-----------|----------|
| **1. Double Cleanse** | SNOW O₂ CLEANSER (500 ml) | O₂ bubble cleanse | 5 min |
| **2. Tone** | SNOW BOOSTER (1000 ml) | pH balancing | 2 min |
| **3. Gentle Exfoliation** | EPI TURNOVER BOOSTING PEELING GEL | Apply to pigmented zones, massage gently in circular motions until dead cells roll off, rinse | 5 min |
| **4. Carboxy Therapy** | EZ CO₂ MASK KIT | Full face application — CO₂ triggers Bohr effect, increasing O₂ delivery to hyperpigmented tissue | 15–20 min |
| **5. Microneedling** | POWER SOLUTION **SWS** (1–2 vials) | Apply SWS ampoule. Needle at **0.25–0.5 mm** only. Focus passes on pigmented areas with even, overlapping strokes. Avoid aggressive cross-hatching on dark spots | 15–20 min |
| **6. Post Mask** | BIO-FERMENT AGE DEFYING POWDER MASK | Mix with water to paste, apply evenly, leave on for concentrated nutrient and brightening delivery | 15 min |
| **7. Finish** | SKIN RESCUE OVERNIGHT CREAM MASK | Oxygen capsules burst on skin → pink ceramide cream seals and calms | 2 min |
| **8. SPF** | ULTRA SHIELD SUN CREAM SPF 50+ | Mandatory — pigmentation patients must not leave without SPF | 1 min |

### Why This Works

- **EPI TURNOVER PEELING GEL** gently removes the pigmented stratum corneum (enzymatic papaya + cellulose action) without aggressive acid exposure
- **Carboxy** oxygenates tissue and has clinically tested brightening effect
- **SWS** delivers the triple-threat of melanin inhibition: **Arbutin** (tyrosinase inhibitor), **Kojic Acid** (chelates copper needed for melanogenesis), and **Licorice Root Extract** (anti-inflammatory depigmenting)
- Shallow needling ensures the actives reach melanocytes without causing deep-dermal inflammation that could trigger rebound PIH

| Time | Product | Application |
|------|---------|-------------|
| Morning | MULTI VITA RADIANCE SERUM | Contains MELAZERO® patented melanin-care complex + stable Vitamin C derivative |
| Morning | ULTRA SHIELD SUN CREAM SPF 50+ | **Non-negotiable** — reapply every 2 hours. Emphasize this to the patient |

| **4. Carboxy Therapy** | EZ CO₂ MASK KIT | Calms post-peel inflammation while oxygenating freshly exfoliated skin | 15–20 min |
| **5. Microneedling** | Selected POWER SOLUTION (1 vial) | Apply ampoule. Needle at **0.5 mm only** — the peel has already thinned the stratum corneum. Gentle, even passes. Less pressure than standalone microneedling | 10–15 min |

- **Light microneedling** (0.5 mm) creates channels through the newly thinned stratum corneum — dramatically enhancing ampoule absorption

## Home Care Programs

### Universal Post-Microneedling Rules (All Protocols)

**Day 0 (Treatment Day):**
- No washing face for 6–8 hours
- No makeup for 12–24 hours
- No active ingredients (retinol, AHA/BHA, vitamin C) for 48 hours
- Sleep on clean pillowcase (ideally silk)

**Days 1–3:**
- Gentle cleanser only (SNOW O₂ CLEANSER)
- ALL FOR SENSITIVE SERUM + SOOTHING REPAIR POSTCREAM
- SPF 50+ mandatory, reapply every 2 hours if outdoors
- No gym, sauna, swimming pool, or direct sun

**Day 4 onwards:**
- Resume concern-specific home care (see individual protocols above)
- Continue strict sun protection until next session

## Contraindications & Safety

### Absolute Contraindications (Do NOT Treat)

- Active skin infections (bacterial, viral, fungal) in treatment area
- Active herpes simplex outbreak (prescribe prophylactic antivirals if history of cold sores)
- Uncontrolled diabetes
- Active chemotherapy or radiation therapy
- Blood clotting disorders or anticoagulant therapy
- Pregnancy and breastfeeding
- Active cystic/nodular acne on treatment site (for microneedling — carboxy alone is safe)
- Keloidal scarring tendency
- Isotretinoin (Accutane) use within the last 6 months

### Relative Contraindications (Proceed with Caution)

- Rosacea (avoid carboxy, use HES ampoule only, shallow depth)
- Eczema/psoriasis (avoid flared areas)
- Recent cosmetic procedures (filler — wait 2 weeks; Botox — wait 1 week)
- Autoimmune conditions (consult physician)
- Sensitive skin (start with 0.25 mm, single ampoule, no SRS)

### Emergency Kit (Keep in Treatment Room)

- Sterile saline for eye flush
- Antihistamine cream (in case of allergic reaction)
- Ice packs
- Chlorhexidine solution
- Emergency contact numbers posted visibly
