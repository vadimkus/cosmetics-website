# Product 27 — use on children — 2026-09-05

Question: can Skin Barrier Protecting Cream be used on children, from what age?

## Answer

**Do not recommend. No age is approved.**

Carton and SA do not give a starting age. Category is face cream / skin care, not a baby or pediatric product. “Keep out of children’s reach” is storage, not permission to use.

## Sources

- Label / Artwork: external use, avoid eyes, cool dry place, keep out of children’s reach, stop and see a doctor if irritation.
- SA (QACS, 15 Dec 2020, Amendment I): safe under normal adult use. Rosemary in MultiEx *may* carry camphor / eucalyptol; literature recommends no camphor / eucalyptol products for children under 3. Scutellaria literature line: avoid use by children. MultiEx itself is 0.0001%, so those botanicals are trace, but HQ never cleared pediatric use.
- Fragrance: mild lavender 0.015%, linalool + coumarin on pack.

Do not invent “from 12” or similar. Dermatologist if a parent still wants it on an older child.
