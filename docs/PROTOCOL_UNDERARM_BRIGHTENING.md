## 3. Step-by-Step Procedure

### Step 1: Cleansing & Preparation (5 minutes)

**Product:** SNOW O₂ CLEANSER + SNOW BOOSTER

1. **Apply SNOW O₂ CLEANSER** to the dry underarm area using gentle, even strokes.
2. **Wait** for the naturally generated oxygen bubbles to form. The oxygen therapy mechanism lifts away deodorant residue, oils, and impurities from within the skin folds.
   - *Why this product:* The oxygen-bubble technology eliminates the need for excessive rubbing — critical for the sensitive axillary folds where friction can trigger further pigmentation (post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation).
3. **Wipe clean** with a damp, lukewarm sponge. Pat the area dry.
4. **Apply SNOW BOOSTER** with a cotton pad, pressing gently along the underarm skin.
   - *Why this product:* The Lactobacillus/Pumpkin Ferment Extract and Betaine restore pH balance after cleansing, while Phytolex SC and Lotus Flower Extract provide initial soothing and hydration, preparing the skin for optimal absorption of active ingredients in subsequent steps.

### Step 2: Gentle Exfoliation (3–5 minutes)

**Product:** EPI TURNOVER BOOSTING PEELING GEL

1. **Apply a thin, even layer** of the peeling gel to the underarm area.
2. **Gently massage** in circular motions until cellulose rolls form, collecting dead skin cells and superficial pigmented cells.
   - *Why this product:* This gel uses a dual-action approach — **enzymatic peeling** (Papaya Fruit Extract breaks down keratin bonds) combined with **physical cellulose peeling** (gentle rolling action). Moringa Pterygosperma Seed Extract ("Miracle Tree") purifies and nourishes, while the Desert Complex and Hyaluronic Acid ensure the exfoliated skin remains hydrated and soothed rather than irritated.
3. **Wipe away thoroughly** with a damp sponge. Ensure no residue remains before proceeding.

### Step 3: Oxygenation & Active Brightening (15–20 minutes)

**Product:** EZ CO₂ MASK KIT (Gel + Sheet)

1. **Apply the EZ CO₂ GEL** evenly over the underarm area.
2. **Place the EZ CO₂ MASK sheet** over the gel and press firmly to initiate the carboxy reaction.
3. **The Bohr Effect activates:** Fine CO₂ particles generated by the contact between gel (containing Lactic Acid) and mask (containing Sodium Bicarbonate) cause localized vasodilation and force hemoglobin to release bound oxygen directly into the skin tissues.
   - *Why this product:* The EZ CO₂ MASK KIT is clinically tested specifically for **improving skin roughness and hyperpigmentation**. The botanical complex in both gel and mask — Licorice Root Extract (Glycyrrhiza Glabra), Centella Asiatica, Green Tea, Rosemary, and Chamomilla — delivers anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and melanin-inhibiting benefits directly to the oxygenated tissue. Portulaca Oleracea Extract provides additional soothing for sensitive axillary skin.
4. **Leave on for 15–20 minutes.** Remove the mask sheet and cleanse the area thoroughly with a damp sponge.

### Step 4: Targeted Nutrient Masking (15 minutes)

**Product:** BIO-FERMENT AGE DEFYING POWDER MASK

1. **Mix the powder mask with water** (follow the recommended ratio on the packaging) to activate the fermentation-derived formula.
2. **Apply the paste evenly** over the underarms using a spatula or gloved hands.
   - *Why this product:* The fermentation technology breaks down active compounds into smaller, more bioavailable molecules that penetrate more effectively into the skin. This delivers concentrated nutrients for repair, brightening, and smoothing of the underarm area. The powder-to-mask activation ensures maximum freshness and potency at the moment of application.
3. **Leave on for 15 minutes.** The mask will set and firm.
4. **Gently peel off** the mask and wipe clean with a damp sponge.

### Step 5: Finishing & Post-Treatment Care (2–3 minutes)

**Product:** SKIN RESCUE OVERNIGHT CREAM MASK

1. **Apply a generous layer** of the cream mask to the underarm area.
2. **Massage lightly** until the oxygen capsules burst and the product is absorbed.
   - *Why this product:* This cream mask features a unique **dual formula** — Italian oxygenated water encapsulated in oxygen capsules that burst on contact, melting into a Pink Ceramide cream base. The Growth Factor Complex (EGF, aFGF, bFGF, PIGF, IGF) accelerates skin recovery. Phytosphingosine reinforces the lipid barrier. The Swelling Controller and Pumpkin Fruit Extract calm any residual redness. This product is clinically tested for **improving erythema and transepidermal water loss (TEWL)** — both critical markers for post-treatment axillary skin.
3. **Do not rinse.** Allow the product to continue working as a leave-on treatment.

## 5. Post-Treatment Advice for the Client

### Immediate Aftercare (First 24–48 Hours)

- **Deodorant:** Avoid all deodorants, antiperspirants, perfumed products, and shaving for at least **24–48 hours** post-treatment.
- **Clothing:** Wear loose-fitting, breathable fabrics (cotton preferred) to minimize friction-induced hyperpigmentation.
- **Water contact:** Avoid hot water, swimming pools, and saunas for 24 hours. Lukewarm water is acceptable.
- **Sun exposure:** If the underarm area will be exposed (e.g., sleeveless clothing), apply a broad-spectrum SPF 30+ sunscreen.

### Daily Home Care Regimen (Between Sessions)

| Time | Product | Application |
|------|---------|-------------|
| **Morning** | MULTI VITA RADIANCE SERUM | Apply 2–3 drops to clean, dry underarm skin. Allow to absorb fully before dressing. The MELAZERO® complex and stable Vitamin C derivative work throughout the day to inhibit melanin production. |
| **Evening** | ALL FOR SENSITIVE SERUM | Apply 2–3 drops to clean underarm skin after showering. The MultiEx BSASM® Plus and Phytosphingosine rebuild the moisture barrier and soothe any inflammation from daily friction. |

### Long-Term Maintenance (After Completing the 4–6 Session Course)

- Continue the home care regimen with MULTI VITA RADIANCE SERUM and ALL FOR SENSITIVE SERUM.
- Schedule a single maintenance session every **4–6 weeks** to sustain results.
- Consider adding **MULTI VITA RADIANCE CREAM** (50g) as an evening moisturizer for enhanced brightening maintenance, alternating with ALL FOR SENSITIVE SERUM based on skin condition.

## 6. Contraindications & Precautions

- **Do not perform** if there is active skin infection, open wounds, or active dermatitis in the treatment area.
- **Patch test** recommended for first-time clients, especially on sensitized or reactive skin.
- **Pregnancy/Nursing:** Consult with the supervising physician before proceeding.
- **Laser/Waxing:** Allow at least 1 week between laser hair removal or waxing and this protocol.
