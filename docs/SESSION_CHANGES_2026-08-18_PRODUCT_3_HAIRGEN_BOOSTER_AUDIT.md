## ★ The leaflet is the strongest claim document found in this line

It is subtitled **"Automicroneedling LED Device for Alopecia Treatment"** and opens a
section headed **"Clinical Trials"** with before/after photographs captioned
**"Alopecia areata"** (after 3 months, after 8 months) and **"Androgenic alopecia"**
(after 6 months, after 3 months).

**Alopecia areata is an autoimmune disease.** Presenting a cosmetic device as its
treatment, with clinical photographs, is a different order of claim from anything else
found this week.

The rest of the leaflet:

- *"developed to be used with GENOSYS HR³ MATRIX HAIR SOLUTION α for **anti-hair loss
  treatment**"*
- *"Hairgen Booster **improves hair loss** by promoting **new vessel formation** and blood
  circulation"*
- *"helps to deliver active ingredients … that promote **angiogenesis and vasodilation**"*
- a **"Wound healing process"** section claiming microneedling stimulates *"natural
  collagen and elastin production"* and *"angiogenesis, new vessel formation → more oxygen
  and nutrients delivery to hair follicles"*
- LED claims: *"Stimulate **anagen re-entry** to telogen hair follicles"*, *"Prolong
  duration of anagen phase"*, *"Increase rates of proliferation in active anagen hair
  follicles"*, *"Prevent premature catagen development"*
- and, for the Hair Solution: *"**Inibition of 5α-reductase**, the key enzyme causing male
  hair loss"* (sic), *"Improvement in blood circulation by angiogenesis and vasodilation"*,
  with **VEGF** named first in the key-ingredient list

That is the same 5α-reductase claim already logged on the tonic's Russian panel and on
three slides of the Hair Solution deck. **Running total for this line: eight documents.**

## What is genuinely documented, and good

The device has real, checkable numbers — which is what a page should be built on:

| | |
|---|---|
| Microneedles | **52** per stamp (leaflet, "52EA") |
| LEDs | **14 lights dispersed through 48 light bumps** |
| LED colours | Blue and red |
| Speeds | Level 1 **280 RPM** · Level 2 **330 RPM** · Level 3 **400 RPM** |
| Run time | **10 minutes**, then it stops automatically |
| Consumables | **A fresh solution vial and a fresh stamp every session** |
| Power | 5.0 V DC / 2.0 A; charger rated 5 V, 1–2 A |
| Warranty | **24 months** from purchase |
| Sensation | *"No pain during treatment — massaging sensation instead of needling sensation"* |

**Needle depth is not stated in either document.** This matters, because the corrections
log entry for product 64 already flags that the depth on that page is unsourced. It also
notes "the only needle depth in official artwork is 0.5 mm and it belongs to the flat
derma stamp in the MESOPECIA kit" — **that entry needs correcting**: the Mesopecia kit
contains a **roller**, not a flat stamp (established 18 Aug from the artwork and the
product photograph). So the 0.5 mm belongs to the kit's roller, and the HairGen stamp's
depth remains undocumented.

### Contraindications, from the manual, absent from our site

Four, and only complete if you read across three language panels:

1. Progressive acne, eczema or any dermatitis
2. **Complications of diabetes or other serious illness**
3. **Keloid-prone skin or metal allergy**
4. Inflamed areas or areas at risk of infection

Plus: stop and seek medical advice on rash or allergic reaction, and do not use the device
with cosmetics other than those the manufacturer recommends — which is a real constraint
on a device that opens channels in skin.

## Recommended handling when the page is built

1. **Lead on the specification**, as product 49's page does: 52 needles, 14 LEDs, three
   speeds, the 10-minute cutoff, single-use consumables per session. It is a device; the
   dosimetry-style table is the page.
2. **Follow the English panel of nothing.** Use the Korean/German/Chinese framing — scalp
   care — since that is also the framing decision already applied to products 43–47.
3. **Never carry** alopecia treatment, alopecia areata, angiogenesis, vasodilation,
   anagen/telogen/catagen mechanics, 5α-reductase, or the before/after clinical photos.
4. **Put the four contraindications on the page**, and the running cost: a session needs a
   fresh 4 ml vial (AED 92.50) and a fresh stamp (AED 75, from AED 600 per box of eight),
   so roughly **AED 167 per session in consumables** on top of the device.
5. Note the honest relationship to product 47: the Mesopecia kit is the manual version of
   the same idea at AED 1,100, this is the powered one at AED 1,800 plus consumables.
