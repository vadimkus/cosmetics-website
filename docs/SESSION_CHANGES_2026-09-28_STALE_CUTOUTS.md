# Stale cut-outs: products 3, 16, 37, 53

Date: 2026-09-28.

## Fault

The closing band on /products/3 showed the retired HairGen glassware photograph under the new
headline. `lib/productCutouts.ts` mapped the current main (`hairgen_campaign/main2.jpg`) to
`cutout/3.webp`, which had been traced from the old shot, so every surface that prefers the
cut-out (closing band, cards, bag) showed the wrong product.

A check of all 66 pairs for a source photo newer than its cut-out found three more:

| # | Main | Old cut-out showed |
|---|---|---|
| 16 SNOW BOOSTER | `booster_campaign/main.jpg`, 1000 ml + 200 ml on snow | the 200 ml alone |
| 37 PEPTIDE GEL MASK | `peptide_campaign/main.jpg`, sachet + box | the sachet alone |
| 53 COLLAGEN MASK | `collagen_campaign/main.jpg` | the earlier sachet shot |

## Fix

New files under new names (`/images/*` is immutable for a year): `3-v2`, `16-v2`, `37-v2`, `53-v2`.

- **3**: traced from main2.jpg. Glossy black floor, so `FLOOR` now takes per-column spans:
  carton 83.2%, device 84.1% (the LED tips stand lower). Vision tore out the carton's silver
  side panel behind the device; restored whole from the photo by `REPAIR`.
- **16**: the bottles stand buried in snow and Vision left a crumbled base. v2 is the two supplied
  container PNGs placed at the main's measured scale and position, then normalised, like 39, 40
  and 46. Do not rebuild 16 from the photograph.
- **37**: an ice cube stands over the sachet's bottom seal and Vision cut a V into the pack. New
  `CLONE` repair rebuilds the region from the same rows to its left and shifts each row's colour
  to meet both sides of the gap. The ice itself is dropped.
- **53**: traced from the current main; the water drops are dropped.

Checked on the band grey with the page's drop shadow before shipping. `lib/productCutouts.ts`
regenerated by `write-manifest.py`; only the four entries changed.
