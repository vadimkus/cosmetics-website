# Product 25 — SOOTHING REPAIR POSTCREAM — source audit

The post-procedure cream, and commercially one of the more important products in
the range because it is what clinics hand to a client after microneedling.

**Unusually, this is a re-attribution rather than a debunking.** The soothing
claim is genuinely well supported. It is just supported by completely different
ingredients from the ones our record leads with.

## Our key-ingredient list is in almost exactly inverse order of concentration

The record reads: *"Key ingredients: sh-Polypeptide-7, Centella Complex
(Asiaticoside, Madecassic Acid, Asiatic Acid), Dipotassium Glycyrrhizate,
Panthenol, Vitis Vinifera (Grape) Callus Culture Extract, Rosa Damascena Callus
Culture Extract, Scutellaria Baicalensis Root Extract."*

Against the formula:

| Position in our list | Ingredient | Actual concentration |
|---|---|---|
| **1st** | **sh-Polypeptide-7** | **0.000001% = 10 parts per billion** |
| 2nd | Centella triterpenes, combined | 0.020% = 200 ppm |
| 3rd | **Dipotassium glycyrrhizate** | **0.200% — a proper dose** |
| 4th | Panthenol | 0.050% — low |
| 5th | Grape callus culture extract | 0.006% = 60 ppm |
| 6th | Rosa damascena callus culture extract | 0.006% = 60 ppm |
| **7th** | **Scutellaria baicalensis root extract** | **0.200% — a proper dose** |

The two ingredients genuinely at anti-inflammatory doses sit at positions **3 and
7**, while a peptide at **ten parts per billion leads the list**. And the largest
functional component of the product is not mentioned anywhere.

## What actually makes this a good post-procedure cream

**18.4% humectants.** Butylene glycol at **12.000%** and glycerin at **6.390%**.
For skin that has just been through microneedling or a laser, water-binding at
that level is the single most useful thing a cream can do, and it is the reason
the specific gravity comes in just **above** water at 1.004–1.009, unlike the
other creams audited this week.

**A genuine anti-inflammatory set, all at working doses:**

| Ingredient | Dose | |
|---|---|---|
| Dipotassium glycyrrhizate | **0.200%** | Licorice-derived, standard cosmetic use 0.1–0.5% |
| Scutellaria baicalensis root extract | **0.200%** | The baicalin source |
| Allantoin | **0.200%** | |
| Tocopheryl acetate | **0.500%** | Vitamin E |
| Arginine | **0.500%** | |
| Bisabolol | 0.050% | Real, at the low end of typical use |

**A moderate lipid phase to seal it in:** dimethicone 2.000%, squalane 1.500%,
caprylic/capric triglyceride 1.500%, cetearyl olivate 0.900%, cetearyl alcohol
0.800%, sorbitan olivate 0.600%, jojoba 0.500%, beeswax 0.500%.

That is a properly built barrier-support cream. Nothing about it needed a
10-ppb peptide on the front of the list.

## The Centella complex, handled precisely

Asiaticoside 0.008%, madecassic acid 0.006%, asiatic acid 0.006% — **0.020%
combined**. These are **purified triterpenes rather than a crude extract**, so it
is a deliberate and reasonably expensive ingredient choice, not label dressing.
But the wound-healing literature on asiaticoside works at 0.1% to 1%, so this is
somewhere between five and fifty times lower. Present and worth naming; not a
therapeutic dose. The page says exactly that.

## Claims that have to go

**"Efficacy test on protection of the skin against damage induced by physical
stimuli."** No such report is anywhere on the drive, and the 42-page safety
assessment does not reference it — the string "physical stimuli" does not appear
in it at all. Removed.

**"Regenerating cream… promotes healthy rejuvenation."** The registered carton
says **"Function Soothing"** and nothing else. There is no Korean functional
licence on this product at all — no `기능성화장품` bracket on the Korean panel,
unlike products 32, 23 and 42 — and correspondingly **neither certificate of
analysis carries an ingredient assay line**, because there is no functional active
to assay. What the carton does support is *"SRP helps fast skin recovery after
professional treatment"*, which is the manufacturer's own registered claim and is
what the page uses.

**The callus culture extracts as a regeneration story.** 60 ppm each. The same
treatment already applied to product 24, where callus and stem-cell language was
cut.

**"After the dermatological operations."** See below — this needs care, not just
softening.

## The carton says to avoid broken skin, and this matters

Korean precaution 2 reads: **상처가 있는 부위 등에는 사용을 자제할 것** — *refrain
from use on wounded or broken areas of skin.*

That is a significant nuance for a product sold as a post-procedure cream. It is
for skin that is **irritated, red or recovering**, not for open wounds. Our
description said it helps skin recover "after the dermatological operations",
which a reader could easily take as permission to apply it to broken skin. The
page now states the distinction plainly and tells people to follow their
practitioner's instruction on timing.

## Things the carton says that our record never did

- **Period after opening: 6 months.** The `6M` symbol is on the carton. For a
  100 g professional tube being used across many clients in a treatment room,
  this is genuinely useful information and it was nowhere on our site.
- **Beeswax 0.500%**, so not vegan. The safety assessment identifies it as
  "purified wax from the honeycomb of the bee, Apis mellifera".
- **Linalool 0.0047%**, correctly declared on this carton — unlike product 23.
  The assessment goes further and gives an **analytically measured** value from
  QACS of **0.0032%** in the finished product, rather than only the calculated
  figure. Worth noting: they actually tested for the allergen.
- **It may be applied several times a day.** The carton's own application
  instruction: *"If necessary (Depending on your needs), apply the product several
  times a day."* That is a reassuring and clinically sensible permission, and it
  belongs in the how-to.
- **Avoid during pregnancy and lactation** — already on our record, correctly.

## The safety assessment is the cleanest of the week

> **1. ASSESSMENT CONCLUSION** — The product is considered **safe for human
> health** when used under normal or reasonably foreseeable conditions of use.

No "with restrictions" qualifier, unlike products 32 and 23. Patch test graded
**"Non Irritant" by QACS Ltd**, clearing "dermatologically tested".
