## 3. Step-by-Step Procedure

### Step 1: Cleansing & Preparation (5 minutes)

**Product:** SNOW O₂ CLEANSER + SNOW BOOSTER

1. **Apply SNOW O₂ CLEANSER** to the dry underarm area using gentle, even strokes.
2. **Wait** for the naturally generated oxygen bubbles to form. The oxygen therapy mechanism lifts away deodorant residue, oils, and impurities from within the skin folds.
   - *Why this product:* The oxygen-bubble technology (powered by Methyl Perfluoroisobutyl Ether) eliminates the need for excessive rubbing — critical for the sensitive axillary folds where friction itself triggers post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation. Phytolex SC and MultiEx Phytrogen provide immediate soothing. All GENOSYS cleansers are dermatologically tested.
3. **Wipe clean** with a damp, lukewarm sponge. Pat the area dry.
4. **Apply SNOW BOOSTER** with a cotton pad, pressing gently along the underarm skin.
   - *Why this product:* The Lactobacillus/Pumpkin Ferment Extract supports the skin's microbiome (important in the axillary area which has a unique microbial environment). Betaine and Nelumbo Nucifera (Lotus) Flower Extract restore pH balance after cleansing, while Phytolex SC provides soothing — preparing the skin for optimal penetration of active ingredients in subsequent steps. Clinically tested for skin hydration.

### Step 2: Gentle Exfoliation (3–5 minutes)

**Product:** EPI TURNOVER BOOSTING PEELING GEL

1. **Apply a thin, even layer** of the peeling gel to the underarm area.
2. **Gently massage** in circular motions until cellulose rolls form, collecting dead skin cells and superficial pigmented cells.
   - *Why this product:* This gel uses a dual-action approach:
     - **Enzymatic peeling** — Carica Papaya (Papaya) Fruit Extract contains papain enzyme that breaks down keratin bonds holding dead, pigmented cells to the surface.
     - **Cellulose peeling** — gentle physical rolling action lifts loosened cells without micro-tears or harsh friction.
     - Moringa Pterygosperma Seed Extract ("Miracle Tree") purifies and nourishes, while the Desert Complex and Hyaluronic Acid ensure the freshly exfoliated skin remains hydrated and soothed. Jojoba Seed Oil provides a protective lipid layer.
   - *Why not SKIN RENEWAL PEELING SYSTEM (SRS)?* While the SRS professional peel (containing Glycolic Acid, Lactic Acid, Mandelic Acid, Phytic Acid) would offer stronger chemical exfoliation and brightening, the EPI TURNOVER is the safer choice for the axillary area due to its non-irritating enzymatic approach. The underarm skin is thinner, more reactive, and prone to post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation — a milder peel prevents the rebound darkening that aggressive AHA peels can cause in this area. *The SRS could be considered for advanced sessions (session 5–6) on clients showing good tolerance, at the clinician's discretion.*
3. **Wipe away thoroughly** with a damp sponge. Ensure no residue remains before proceeding.

### Step 3: Oxygenation & Active Brightening (15–20 minutes)

**Product:** EZ CO₂ MASK KIT (Gel + Sheet)

1. **Apply the EZ CO₂ GEL** evenly over the underarm area.
2. **Place the EZ CO₂ MASK sheet** over the gel and press firmly to initiate the carboxy reaction.
3. **The Bohr Effect activates:** Fine CO₂ particles generated by the contact between gel (Lactic Acid base) and mask (Sodium Bicarbonate base) cause localized vasodilation. The elevated CO₂ concentration forces hemoglobin to release bound oxygen directly into the skin tissues.
   - *Why this product:* The EZ CO₂ MASK KIT is **clinically tested specifically for improving skin roughness and hyperpigmentation**. This is the single most important product in the protocol for the underarm brightening indication. The botanical complex in both gel and mask delivers multiple anti-pigment mechanisms simultaneously:
     - **Glycyrrhiza Glabra (Licorice) Root Extract** — contains Glabridin, a potent tyrosinase inhibitor that blocks melanin production
     - **Lactic Acid** — mild AHA that promotes cell turnover and has independent skin-lightening properties
     - **Centella Asiatica Extract** — promotes wound healing and reduces inflammation-driven pigmentation
     - **Camellia Sinensis (Green Tea) Leaf Extract** — antioxidant that protects against oxidative-stress-driven melanogenesis
     - **Portulaca Oleracea Extract** — powerful anti-inflammatory, ideal for soothing the sensitive axillary area
     - **Scutellaria Baicalensis Root Extract** — anti-inflammatory with skin-brightening properties (Baicalin)
     - **Chamomilla Recutita (Matricaria) Flower Extract** — calming and anti-inflammatory
     - **Rosmarinus Officinalis (Rosemary) Leaf Extract** — antioxidant protection
4. **Leave on for 15–20 minutes.** Remove the mask sheet and cleanse the area thoroughly with a damp sponge.

The SWS contains **Arbutin** (inhibits tyrosinase), **Kojic Acid** (blocks melanin synthesis), **Glycyrrhiza Uralensis (Licorice) Root Extract** (Glabridin), and **Aspergillus/Saccharomyces/Glycyrrhiza Uralensis Root Extract Ferment Filtrate** (fermented brightening complex). ## 4. Scientific Rationale: Why This Combination Works

### The Underarm Pigmentation Problem

Underarm hyperpigmentation is driven by a cycle of:
1. **Friction** (clothing, skin-on-skin contact) → triggers inflammation
2. **Inflammation** → stimulates melanocytes → post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH)
3. **Shaving/hair removal** → micro-trauma → more inflammation → more PIH
4. **Occlusion** (closed skin fold, deodorant) → trapped irritants → chronic low-grade inflammation
5. **Compromised barrier** → increased TEWL → skin vulnerability → cycle repeats

## 5. Post-Treatment Advice for the Client

### Immediate Aftercare (First 24–48 Hours)

- **Deodorant:** Avoid all deodorants, antiperspirants, and perfumed products for at least **24–48 hours** post-treatment.
- **Hair removal:** No shaving, waxing, or depilatory creams for **48 hours** minimum.
- **Clothing:** Wear loose-fitting, breathable fabrics (cotton preferred) to minimize friction-induced hyperpigmentation rebound.
- **Water contact:** Avoid hot water, swimming pools, and saunas for 24 hours. Lukewarm water is fine.
- **Sun exposure:** If underarm area will be exposed (sleeveless clothing), apply broad-spectrum SPF 30+.

### Daily Home Care Regimen (Between Sessions)

| Time | Product | Application |
|------|---------|-------------|
| **Morning** | **MULTI VITA RADIANCE SERUM** (30ml) | Apply 2–3 drops to clean, dry underarm skin. Allow to absorb fully before dressing. The MELAZERO® complex, stable Vitamin C derivative, Niacinamide, and Glutathione work via four independent pathways to inhibit melanin production throughout the day. **Clinically tested for improving skin surface melanin.** |
| **Evening** | **ALL FOR SENSITIVE SERUM** (30ml) | Apply 2–3 drops to clean underarm skin after showering. The MultiEx BSASM® Plus, Phytosphingosine, Hyaluronic Acid, Aloe, Witch Hazel, and Beta-Glucan rebuild the moisture barrier and soothe any inflammation caused by daily friction. |

**Optional enhancement:** Layer **MULTI VITA RADIANCE CREAM** (50g) over the evening serum (on nights when the ALL FOR SENSITIVE is not needed) for additional MELAZERO® exposure and a moisturizing seal. Uses the same active complex as the serum in a cream vehicle.

### Long-Term Maintenance (After 4–6 Session Course)

- Continue the home care regimen with MULTI VITA RADIANCE SERUM and ALL FOR SENSITIVE SERUM.
- Schedule a single maintenance session every **4–6 weeks** to sustain results.
- For stubborn cases, consider incorporating POWER SOLUTION SWS into maintenance sessions.

## 7. Contraindications & Precautions

- **Do not perform** if there is active skin infection, open wounds, or active dermatitis in the treatment area.
- **Patch test** recommended for first-time clients, especially on sensitized or reactive skin.
- **Pregnancy/Nursing:** Consult with the supervising physician before proceeding.
- **Laser/Waxing:** Allow at least 1 week between laser hair removal or waxing and this protocol.
- **Botulinum toxin (for hyperhidrosis):** Can be combined; no contraindication, but schedule the protocol at least 48 hours after injection.
