## Core Diagnosis

The GENOSYS UAE Instagram currently appears clean and medical, but too much like a product catalogue or distributor feed. It underuses the strongest trust-building assets in aesthetic skincare: real procedures, real specialists, real skin, before/after evidence, protocol education, clinic context, and human stories.

The account needs to move from "we sell products" to "we are the UAE clinical skincare platform for microneedling, PDRN, recovery and professional protocols."

## Positioning

Recommended profile direction:

```text
GENOSYS UAE
Advanced Korean Clinical Skincare
Microneedling • PDRN • Skin Recovery
Official UAE Distributor | Dubai
Products • Training • Clinic Supply
```

Russian-language positioning:

```text
Профессиональная корейская косметология в UAE
Microneedling • PDRN • восстановление кожи
Официальный дистрибьютор GENOSYS
Продукты • обучение • поставки для клиник
```

Use one clear hierarchy: professional clinical skincare first, official distributor second, product/training/clinic supply third.

## Content Pillars

1. Proof and trust:
   - before/after results where permission is available
   - short doctor/cosmetologist comments
   - procedure room content
   - clinic partner content
   - real client recovery timelines

2. Education:
   - what microneedling does
   - why PDRN is used
   - how skin recovery works after procedures
   - which products are professional vs home care
   - common mistakes after treatments

3. Product-to-protocol:
   - stop posting isolated product cards
   - show product role inside a complete protocol
   - explain when, why, and for whom each product is used

4. Human brand:
   - founder/distributor notes
   - behind the scenes
   - training moments
   - clinic deliveries and partner stories
   - Russian-language warm expert copy, not generic catalogue copy

5. Dubai / UAE context:
   - premium lifestyle, clinics, sun exposure, skin recovery, expat audience
   - professional care in Dubai Marina / UAE
   - local availability and support

## Russian Copy Direction

The Russian audience should receive warmer, more human copy than the current catalogue style. The "Инструкция по эксплуатации" file is not a GENOSYS content strategy document and should not be copied into the brand feed, but it demonstrates a useful tone principle: Russian-language writing performs better when it feels alive, specific, observant and human.

Use this tone in a professional way:

- less "product contains X"
- more "why this matters for skin after procedure"
- less brand slogan
- more expert explanation
- less sterile perfection
- more trust, experience and care

## Main Rule

Every post should answer at least one of these:

- Why does it work?
- Who is it for?
- Is it safe?
- What result can someone expect?
- Who uses it professionally?
- How can a clinic/customer order in UAE?

If a post only shows a product without answering one of these, it is catalogue content and should be rewritten.
