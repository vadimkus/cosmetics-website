## G. TYPOS AND ERRORS

Highest severity first.

### G1. `Объем: 23 г` — wrong unit type (Russian)
`Объем` means **volume**. Grams are **mass**. Should be `Масса: 23 г` or `Вес: 23 г`.

### G2. `EXP. DD/MM/YYYY` vs `EXP. YYYY/MM/DD` — the two pack versions specify opposite date formats
Artwork prints `EXP. DD/MM/YYYY`; the photographed sachet prints `EXP. YYYY/MM/DD`. A batch stamped `01/02/2026` is unreadable across the two.

### G3. Manufacturer contradiction between panels
Korean names ㈜에스엘씨 (SLC Co., Ltd.) as `화장품제조업자` (manufacturer) and DTS MG as `화장품책임판매업자` (responsible seller). Russian names `Изготовитель: DTS MG. Co. Ltd.` — manufacturer. The two panels contradict each other on the same pack.

### G4. `DTS MG. Co. Ltd.` (Russian block) — stray period, wrong comma
Should be `DTS MG Co., Ltd.` as printed in the English block. The period after `MG` is wrong and the comma after `Co` is missing.

### G5. `Büyükdere Cad. 193 No: 193` (Turkish) — house number printed twice
Should read `Büyükdere Cad. No: 193`.

### G6. Arabic product name omits `REPAIR`
`انتنسيف كولاجين ماسك` = "Intensive Collagen Mask". Should be `انتنسيف ريبير كولاجين ماسك` or equivalent.

### G7. `гидролизированного` (Russian) — misspelling
Correct Russian is `гидролизованного`. `гидролизированного` is not a standard form.

### G8. `improves skin firmness and protects skin barrier by soothing and hydrating skin` — three English grammar faults in one sentence
1. Sentence fragment with no subject and a lowercase opening: `improves skin firmness...` on the artwork front.
2. `protects skin barrier` is missing the article — should be `protects the skin barrier`.
3. `hydrating skin` is missing the article — should be `hydrating the skin`.

### G9. `Use immediately after open.` — ungrammatical
