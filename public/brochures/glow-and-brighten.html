Science Behind the Glow

Key Active Ingredients

Vitamin C (L-Ascorbic Acid)

Powerful antioxidant that prevents UV-induced pigmentation and brightens existing dark spots at the cellular level

Niacinamide (Vitamin B3)

Inhibits melanosome transfer to skin surface — reduces dark spots, controls sebum, and strengthens barrier

Arbutin

Natural brightening agent that blocks tyrosinase enzyme — gentle yet effective, safe for daily use

AHA / BHA Complex

Accelerates cell turnover, removes melanin-rich dead cells, and improves penetration of brightening serums

Hyaluronic Acid

Holds 1000× its weight in water — deeply hydrates without oil, supporting barrier function for healthier skin

Broad-Spectrum SPF 50+

PA++++ protection blocks both UVA and UVB — prevents new pigmentation from forming in UAE sun

Expected Results

Noticeable lightening of moderate pigmentation. Melasma patches begin to fade. Friends start asking what you've been using.

Significant improvement in overall brightness. Deeper spots start to lighten. Skin tone looks visibly more uniform.

Major fading of pigmentation achieved. Even, radiant skin tone. Transition to maintenance phase with continued daily SPF.
