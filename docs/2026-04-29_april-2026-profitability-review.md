## Executive Summary

April 2026 is profitable. MoySklad shows `122,797 AED` net sales, `36,477 AED` sell cost, and `86,320 AED` gross profit. Gross margin is `70.3%`, which is healthy and essentially unchanged versus April 2025.

The weakness is not margin. The weakness is commercial momentum versus March: lower revenue, fewer sales, and a lower average check.

Against April 2025, the business is larger:

- Net sales are up `5.5%`.
- Gross profit is up `5.4%`.
- Sales count is up `26.7%`.
- Average check is down `16.7%`.

That means the business is doing more transactions for only slightly more money. Operationally, that is busier, not necessarily better.

## What Changed Versus March

April is down versus March because:

1. Sales volume fell from `146` to `133` transactions on a like-for-like basis.
2. Average check fell from `1,024 AED` to `923 AED`.
3. Masks and cleanse / tone / peel categories dropped meaningfully.
4. March had stronger high-ticket category movement.

What did not break:

1. Gross margin improved from `69.2%` to `70.3%`.
2. No sales returns were recorded in April.
3. Cash collection remained strong.
4. April cash net was positive despite Q1 tax and inventory payments.

## What Changed Versus April 2025

April 2026 is better than April 2025 on total business size:

- Revenue is up `5.5%`.
- Gross profit is up `5.4%`.
- Sales count is up `26.7%`.
- Margin is stable.

But average check is down sharply from `1,108 AED` to `923 AED`. That is the key warning. Growth is coming from more, smaller transactions rather than stronger ticket size.

## Recommendations

1. Push high-ticket B2B replenishment in May.
   - Target salons and clinics that ordered in April but did not place a full replenishment order.
   - Priority accounts: Shakirovna, Brau, Evolution, Bianco, First Person, HORTMAN, FACE ROOM, Allure.

2. Build May bundles around the April winners.
   - Peptide Gel Mask + Snow O2 Cleanser + SPF.
   - BB Cushion + SPF + Mist.
   - Radiance Serum + Radiance Cream + SPF.

3. Treat SPF as under-monetized.
   - UAE summer is starting, but SPF is only `7.9k AED` in April.
   - Push SPF50 and SPF40 more aggressively in every May order and salon replenishment.

4. Protect average check.
   - Avoid tiny one-off orders where possible.
   - Push minimum professional reorder packs.
   - Use salon-specific suggested replenishment baskets.

5. Keep margin discipline.
   - There is no evidence that discounts destroyed profitability.
   - Do not solve lower March-to-April revenue by cutting price broadly.
   - Use targeted bundles instead.

## Bottom Line

April 2026 is profitable and healthier than April 2025, but weaker than March 2026. The business is not losing money on product economics. Gross margin is strong at `70.3%`.

The operational issue is average check and category mix. May should focus on larger B2B replenishment orders, SPF seasonality, and high-margin bundle construction rather than broad discounts.
