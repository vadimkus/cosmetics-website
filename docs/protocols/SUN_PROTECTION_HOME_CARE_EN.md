## UAE-Specific Sun Tips

| Tip | Why It Matters |
|-----|---------------|
| Apply SPF 15 minutes before going outdoors | Chemical UV filters need time to bond with skin and activate |
| Don't skip SPF on cloudy days | Up to 80% of UV rays penetrate clouds in the UAE |
| Protect skin while driving | UVA rays pass through car windows — apply to face, neck, and hands |
| Reapply after using a face towel | Towelling removes 50–85% of sunscreen |
| Use SPF even indoors near windows | Office windows transmit UVA which causes photoaging |
| Protect the neck and decolletage | This area shows sun damage first but is often forgotten |
| Store sunscreen below 30°C | Don't leave in the car — heat degrades UV filters |

| Rating | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **PA++++** (ULTRA SHIELD, BB CUSHION) | Highest UVA protection grade — blocks 95%+ of UVA (aging rays) |
| **PA+++** (REVITA GLOW BB) | Very high UVA protection |

| **SPF 40** | Blocks 97.5% of UVB rays |
| **SPF 38** | Blocks 97.4% of UVB rays |
| **SPF 30** | Blocks 96.7% of UVB rays |
