## Understanding Acne in the UAE Climate

The UAE's unique combination of **extreme heat (40°C+)**, **high humidity**, and **constant air conditioning cycling** creates a perfect storm for acne:

- **Heat + humidity** → increased sebum production → clogged pores → breakouts
- **AC cycling** → dehydrated skin surface → barrier damage → rebound oiliness
- **UV exposure** → post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (dark marks after every blemish)
- **Sweat + sunscreen** → occlusion if not cleansed properly → comedonal acne

The GENOSYS Intensive Problem Control system addresses all four factors with a targeted approach that treats acne without destroying the skin barrier.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Over-cleansing** — Washing more than 2× daily strips your barrier and increases oil production
2. **Skipping moisturiser** — Dehydrated skin = more sebum = more breakouts
3. **Skipping SPF** — Every cleared blemish becomes a dark mark without UV protection
4. **Picking/squeezing** — Spreads bacteria, damages tissue, causes scarring
5. **Using harsh scrubs** — Micro-tears in skin = bacteria entry points. Use chemical exfoliants instead
6. **Changing products too often** — Give the system 4–6 weeks before judging results
7. **Using heavy oils/butters** — Even "natural" oils (coconut, shea) can be comedogenic

## UAE-Specific Tips

- **Change pillowcases** every 2–3 days — UAE dust + sebum = bacterial breeding ground
- **Keep face towels separate** and replace daily
- **Don't touch your face** — hands transfer bacteria constantly in public transport and offices
- **Stay hydrated** — drink 2–3 liters of water daily; dehydration triggers more sebum
- **Blot, don't powder** — blotting papers remove oil without clogging; powder + sebum = comedones
- **Clean your phone screen** — phones pressed against cheeks cause "phone acne" along the jawline
