## Understanding Scars in the UAE Climate

Scars form when the skin's healing process is disrupted. Understanding scar types helps you choose the right approach:

| Scar Type | Description | Key Concern |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| **Atrophic** | Depressed, pitted (e.g. ice-pick, boxcar) | Collagen loss during healing |
| **Hypertrophic** | Raised, within original wound boundary | Excess collagen production |
| **PIH** (Post-Inflammatory Hyperpigmentation) | Flat, brown/dark marks | Melanin overproduction after inflammation |
| **PIE** (Post-Inflammatory Erythema) | Flat, red/pink marks | Capillary damage, lingering inflammation |

All scars involve **collagen disruption** — the skin's structural framework is altered. Home care focuses on supporting remodelling, reducing discolouration, and maintaining a healthy barrier.

### Why UAE Conditions Matter for Scars

- **UV exposure** → Scars darken faster than normal skin; PIH worsens dramatically without SPF
- **Air conditioning** → Dries healing skin, impairs barrier function, slows repair
- **Post-procedure care** → After laser, microneedling, or chemical peels, proper home care determines 50%+ of the outcome
- **Heat + sweat** → Can irritate fresh scars; gentle cleansing is essential

The GENOSYS scar protocol addresses these factors with EGF-driven repair, barrier support, and non-negotiable sun protection.

Apply to dry face. Let the oxygen bubbles form naturally — they lift impurities without rubbing or tugging. Scarred skin is often fragile; aggressive cleansing can worsen texture and delay healing.

Apply with a cotton pad or pat into skin with hands. Prepares the skin for better absorption of repair actives and helps balance pH. Gentle enough for scarred and post-procedure skin.

**Product:** MOISTURE REPLENISHING HYALURON SERUM — 330 AED

Apply 2–3 drops to the entire face, concentrating on scarred areas. Hyaluronic acid:

- Supports barrier function — essential in AC-heavy environments
- Improves skin suppleness and prepares it for the next repair step

Apply a thin layer to the full face, with extra attention to scarred zones. Contains **EGF (Epidermal Growth Factor)** which:

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Skipping SPF** — UV is the #1 cause of scar darkening; without daily SPF, PIH can become permanent
2. **Over-exfoliating** — More than 2–3× per week damages the barrier and delays healing
3. **Using harsh scrubs** — Physical scrubs can traumatise scar tissue; use gentle chemical exfoliants
4. **Neglecting moisture** — AC dries skin; dehydrated scars heal slower and look worse
5. **Expecting instant results** — Scar remodelling takes months; consistency is key
6. **Picking at scars** — Re-opens wounds and worsens scarring
7. **Using heavy oils without barrier support** — Occlusive oils alone don't repair; pair with EGF and barrier cream

## UAE-Specific Tips

- **SPF is non-negotiable** — Reapply every 2–3 hours; UV intensity in the UAE accelerates scar darkening
- **Keep scars moisturized in AC** — Barrier cream and hyaluron serum prevent dehydration that slows healing
- **Avoid direct sun on fresh scars** — Wear a hat or seek shade for the first 3–6 months
- **Post-procedure care** — After laser, microneedling, or peels, follow your clinician's instructions and use the barrier cream + EGF mask for accelerated healing
- **Humidity helps** — If possible, use a humidifier at night to counteract AC drying
- **Stay hydrated** — Drink 2–3 litres of water daily; internal hydration supports skin repair
