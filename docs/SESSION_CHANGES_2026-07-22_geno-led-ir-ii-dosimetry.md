## Modes
- RED/BLUE/GREEN/YELLOW + IR: **simultaneous** (confirmed HQ site + How to Use)
- RED + other color: **alternate every 3 seconds** (not classic pulsed duty-cycle)
- Separate total optical output watts (W optical) not published; Rated Power 70 W is electrical.
