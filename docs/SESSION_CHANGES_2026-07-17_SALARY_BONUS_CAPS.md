# Salary & sales-bonus caps for profitability (from 2025)

**Date:** 2026-07-17  
**Source:** MoySklad full year 2025 via `scripts/moysklad-ytd-profitability.js`

## 2025 actual

| Metric | Year AED | Per month |
|---|---:|---:|
| Revenue ex VAT | 1,295,235 | 107,936 |
| Gross profit (68%) | 881,675 | 73,473 |
| Salary | 283,150 | **23,596** |
| Sales bonus | 372,048 | **31,004** |
| Other opex | 248,702 | 20,725 |
| Write-offs | 25,194 | 2,100 |
| **Net clean** | **−47,418** | **−3,952** |

People cost = **74% of gross** — too high. Break-even people budget was ~50.6k/mo; actual was 54.6k/mo.

## Recommendation

| Item | Max / month | Max / year |
|---|---:|---:|
| **Salary** | **22,000** | 264,000 |
| **Sales bonus** | **24,000** | 288,000 |
| **Combined** | **46,000** | 552,000 |

**Operating rule:** bonus = lesser of **24,000** or **25% of that month’s gross profit**, paid only after salary + rent + logistics.

Restated 2025 at these caps → **~+56k net** (instead of −47k).

## Why

At 2025 scale, after other opex + write-offs, only ~46.5k/mo remains for people if you want **~+50k annual profit**.

2026 H1 bonus ran ~**44k/mo** — unsustainable; that is why H1 was −45k.
