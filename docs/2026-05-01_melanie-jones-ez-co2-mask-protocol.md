## What The Treatment Does

The GENOSYS EZ CO2 Mask is a carboxy-style oxygenating mask. The gel and sheet mask work together to create a controlled CO2 reaction on the skin surface. This temporary rise in CO2 encourages the Bohr effect: local carbon dioxide changes the skin environment around the capillaries, helping hemoglobin release more oxygen into the surrounding tissue. The visible result is increased microcirculation, a pink healthy flush, brighter tone, and a fresher, more oxygenated look.

## Before You Start

Use on clean, dry skin only.

Do not apply over open wounds, broken skin, active infection, severe active cystic acne, facial dermatitis, eczema flare-ups, or freshly irritated skin. Avoid direct contact with the eyes, inner nose, lips, and lash line. If your skin is very reactive, test a small area first.

Prepare:

- 1 sachet of EZ CO2 Gel
- 1 EZ CO2 sheet mask
- Spatula or clean brush
- Bowl of cool water
- Soft gauze, sponge, facial cloth, or large cotton pads
- Optional warm damp towel for the final residue removal
- Moisturizer and SPF if used during daytime

## Exact Application Protocol

1. Cleanse the face thoroughly and remove all makeup, sunscreen, and oil.
2. Pat the skin completely dry. The gel must be applied to dry skin.
3. Open one sachet of EZ CO2 Gel.
4. Apply the gel evenly over the whole face with a spatula or clean brush. Use a generous layer. The gel should sit visibly on top of the skin, not disappear like a serum.
5. Avoid the eyelids, direct under-eye lash line, lips, nostrils, and hairline. Keep a small margin around the mouth and eyes.
6. Open one sheet mask pouch and unfold the mask fully.
7. Place the mask over the gel. The inner folded/uncoated side of the sheet mask should touch the gel directly.
8. Press the sheet mask firmly onto the skin with both hands. Make sure it contacts the gel on the forehead, cheeks, nose, chin, and jawline.
9. Once the sheet mask touches the gel, the CO2 reaction starts. Leave the mask on for 5-10 minutes.
10. For a first use or sensitive skin, start with 5 minutes. If the skin tolerates it well, future applications can be left closer to 10 minutes.

## What Melanie Will Feel In The First 10 Seconds

The first seconds are the most active part of the treatment.

0-3 seconds:
The gel feels cool, thick, slippery, and sticky. As soon as the mask is pressed down, the reaction begins.

3-10 seconds:
A strong tingling, prickling, or light burning sensation can appear. This is expected. It may feel like tiny bubbles, warmth, or an oxygen rush under the sheet mask. The skin may start turning pink or red, especially on the cheeks, nose, chin, or areas with thinner skin.

This is the Bohr-effect phase beginning: CO2 is triggering vasodilation and microcirculation, so more oxygen-rich blood is being brought toward the skin surface. The sensation should feel active but tolerable.

Important: mild to strong tingling/burning for the first 10-60 seconds is normal. Severe pain, intense itching, swelling, hives, or burning that keeps getting worse is not normal. If that happens, remove immediately and rinse thoroughly with cool water.

## What Happens During The 5-10 Minutes

After the first 30-60 seconds, the burning/tingling should usually settle into a warm, pulsing, active sensation. The skin may look red under the mask because the capillaries are more active. This temporary redness is part of the oxygenating effect.

During the treatment, the skin is stimulated, microcirculation increases, and the skin surface becomes more receptive to hydration and calming products applied afterward.

## How To Remove The Sticky Gel Properly

The gel is intentionally sticky. Do not try to remove it with dry tissue only, because it will smear and pull on the skin.

1. Slowly peel off the sheet mask from the bottom upward.
2. Do not rub the face immediately. First remove the heavy gel.
3. With a spatula, soft tissue, or cotton pad, gently lift off the thick excess gel from the cheeks, forehead, chin, and nose.
4. Wet gauze, a soft sponge, or a facial cloth with cool water. It should be damp, not dripping.
5. Use a press-and-wipe motion: press the damp cloth onto the sticky area for 3-5 seconds to loosen the gel, then wipe gently outward.
6. Rinse the cloth and repeat 2-3 times until the slippery/sticky film is gone.
7. Around the nose and hairline, use damp cotton pads for better control.
8. If any residue remains, use a warm damp towel for the final pass. Warm is fine; do not use very hot water.
9. Pat the skin dry. Do not scrub.
10. Finish with a calming serum or moisturizer. If used in the morning/daytime, apply SPF.
