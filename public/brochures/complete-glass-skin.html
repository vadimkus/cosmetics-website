The Science of Glass Skin

Key Active Ingredients

Triple-Weight Hyaluronic Acid

Low, medium, and high molecular weights hydrate at three skin depths — holds 1000× its weight in water for full-spectrum plumping

Ceramides

Make up 50% of the skin barrier — restore the lipid structure that prevents transepidermal water loss in AC and desert climate

Panthenol (Vitamin B5)

Attracts moisture, soothes irritation, and accelerates barrier repair — the glass skin multitasker

Pre/Probiotics

Support a healthy skin microbiome — balanced flora equals stronger barrier function and better moisture retention

Glycerin

Powerful humectant that draws moisture from the environment into the skin — present in every hydration layer

Allantoin

Soothes and repairs — calms micro-irritation while supporting the skin's natural healing for a smooth, even canvas
