## Best-practice basis (2026 research)

- Carousels = highest engagement format (~1.92%) and 1.4x more reach than single images.
- Product showcase sweet spot: 5-8 slides. Educational/tutorial: 8-10.
- Format: 1080x1350 (4:5 portrait). One idea per slide. Headline ~10 words.
- Slide 1 hook carries ~80% of success. Final slide = one clear CTA.
- Skincare winning angle: educate-first, soft-sell. Embed product in a routine/how-it-works story.

## Feed strategy (daily rotation)

Rotate three lanes so the feed is not one-note:

1. Pro treatments (PDRN, bio-meso, carboxy, protocols) - aimed at clinics/dermas.
2. Home-care hero products (creams, serums, patches) - aimed at consumers.
3. Education / how to choose (concern to product mapping) - drives saves and shares.

Suggested cadence: 1 pro / 1 home / 1 education post per day, rotating. Save heavy clinical posts for mid-week when clinics plan purchases.

## Reusable 5-slide template (default for every daily product post)

1. Hook - one bold benefit promise (metric-backed when possible for pro products).
2. What it is - name + one-line category + who it is for.
3. How it works - key active or technology in plain language.
4. Proof or benefit - clinical stat, before/after, or top 3 benefits.
5. CTA - where to buy / DM keyword / link in bio.

Variants:
- Lean 4-slide (simple home-care): merge slides 2 + 3.
- Extended 6-slide (pro products): split usage / two-way system into its own slide.
