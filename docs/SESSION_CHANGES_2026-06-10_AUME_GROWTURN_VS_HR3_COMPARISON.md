# Aume "GROW:TURN" Ampoule vs GENOSYS HR³ MATRIX — Competitor Comparison

**Date:** 2026-06-10
**Context:** Vadim received a WhatsApp screenshot of "Aume — Korea's #1 Hair Growth Serum" (tryaume.com) and asked for an honest assessment + ingredient comparison vs GENOSYS hair line.

## Finding 1: Aume is a white-label rebrand of Lilyeve

- Product is the **Lilyeve Grow:Turn Exosome Brush Ampoule** (100ml, 38-pin brush applicator, Barunson Co., Korea).
- Screenshot label shows Lilyeve's trademarked complexes: `(V)BioExo-Water(Heartleaf)` and `SCALP-CLERA`.
- Original retails **~$24–30** (Amazon US $24.65, Jolse $23.76). Aume resells via dropship funnel.
- Aume red flags: "Only 6 left", "sold out 11 times last year", review count inconsistency on same page (13,374 vs 58,374), "visible regrowth in 14 days" / "stops shedding instantly" — biologically implausible claims.
- Verdict: legit product underneath, scammy storefront on top. Buy Lilyeve original if wanted.
