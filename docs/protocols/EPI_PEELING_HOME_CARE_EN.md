## 🌿 About Your Peeling Gel

**EPI TURNOVER BOOSTING PEELING GEL** is a gentle enzymatic peeling gel by Korean skincare brand GENOSYS that delicately removes dead skin cells **without irritation or micro-damage**.

Unlike harsh scrubs and acid peels, EPI TURNOVER works through a dual-action mechanism:

| Mechanism | How It Works |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Enzymatic Peeling** | Papaya extract (papain enzyme) gently dissolves protein bonds between dead cells — no rubbing or skin trauma required |
| **Cellulose Peeling** | During massage, cellulose beads form and gently roll away exfoliated cells from the skin surface |

> 💡 **Result:** Skin instantly becomes smoother, more radiant, and more receptive to subsequent skincare. Active ingredients from serums and creams penetrate deeper and work more effectively.

## 📋 Step-by-Step Application Protocol

### Step 1: Cleanse

Thoroughly cleanse your face to remove makeup and impurities. Your skin should be **clean and dry**.

> 🛒 **We Recommend:** [SNOW O₂ CLEANSER](https://genosys.ae/products/10) (330 AED) — a gentle cleanser with oxygen bubbles. The oxygen foam lifts impurities from pores on its own — no need to rub the skin. The ideal preparation before peeling.

### Step 2: Apply the Peeling Gel

1. **Apply a thin, even layer** of the peeling gel onto dry skin, avoiding the eye and lip area
2. **Wait 30 seconds** to allow the enzymes to activate
3. **Gently massage** in circular motions for 1–2 minutes — you will feel cellulose beads forming as they collect dead skin cells
4. **Pay special attention** to areas with enlarged pores: nose, chin, and forehead (T-zone)

### Step 3: Remove

**Rinse thoroughly** with lukewarm water. Make sure no product residue remains on the skin. Gently pat your face dry with a towel.

### Step 4: Tone

Apply toner to restore your skin's pH balance and prepare it for active skincare.

> 🛒 **We Recommend:** [SNOW BOOSTER](https://genosys.ae/products/16) (260 AED) — a hydrating toner with fermented pumpkin extract and probiotics. Restores pH after peeling, supports the skin microbiome, and preps skin for serum application.

## 🌅 Best Time to Apply

**In the evening** — your skin repairs most actively between 11 PM and 4 AM (cell division rate increases up to 30x!). Peeling in the evening allows your skin to make the most of nighttime renewal.

**The morning after peeling**, always apply sunscreen — freshly exfoliated skin is more sensitive to UV radiation.

## ⚠️ Important Guidelines

- **Do not apply** to damaged skin, open wounds, or active breakouts
- **Avoid** the eye area and mucous membranes
- **Always use SPF** the day after peeling
- **Do not combine** with aggressive acids (AHA/BHA, retinol) on the same evening
- **For first-time use**, apply to a small area of skin to test for any reaction
- **Store** in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight
